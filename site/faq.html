FAQ

Short answers about what IronTeams is and is not.

Questions

Is IronTeams made by Microsoft?

No. It is an unofficial project. Microsoft does not affiliate with, endorse or support it, and “Microsoft Teams” is a trademark of Microsoft Corporation.

Is it allowed at my workplace?

That is for your organization to say. IronTeams talks to the same internal endpoints the Teams web client uses, and your organization’s policies may not allow third-party clients. Check with your IT team before you use it with a work account, and use it at your own risk.

Does it cost anything?

No. IronTeams is free software under the GPL-3.0-or-later license.

How much memory does it use?

About 62 MB with a real work account and a chat with images open, and about 29 MB after 10 minutes idle on the chat list. The Teams web app in a Chromium browser takes around 1100 MB. These are measured numbers from the README; see the chart on the home page for how.

Does it collect data about me?

No telemetry, and nothing is uploaded. The problem report is a local file that you read and attach yourself. This website has no analytics or trackers either.

How does sign-in work, and where is my token kept?

You sign in with a device code on the normal Microsoft sign-in page, and it works with MFA. The refresh token lives in your system keyring, never in a file.

Which systems does it run on?

Linux (used every day), Windows and macOS (tested in CI, not yet tried on real hardware). See Platforms and requirements.

Does it update itself?

The AppImage, Setup.exe and the macOS .pkg do. The RPM, Flatpak, tarball, AUR and cargo builds do not. See Updates.

Can I make calls and join meetings?

There is a native call window with pre-join, meeting controls and the roster, and 1:1 incoming and outgoing call screens. A shared screen gets its own stage with the people in a filmstrip, and full screen shows floating controls. Camera and screen sharing are only compile-tested on Windows and macOS for now.

Can I use more than one account?

Yes. Pick an account in the profile menu and IronTeams restarts on it, after asking if a call, an unsent message or an upload would be cut short. Each account reopens on its own last chat and has its own media cache.

Can I see two chats side by side?

Yes. Choose Open in split view in a chat’s “More chat options” or in the chat list’s right-click menu. The second chat opens right of the first, with its own message box. It needs a wide window, takes chats only and is not kept across a restart.

Can I change the keyboard shortcuts?

Yes, in Settings › Keyboard shortcuts: click the keys of a row and press the new combination. A key another shortcut has, or a plain letter, is refused with the reason. Reset all shortcuts brings the defaults back.

Does it support Arabic?

Yes. Arabic, English and mixed-direction text show in the right direction, each paragraph on its own, and the main screens are translated into Arabic.

Why does it download sounds and pictures from Microsoft?

On first run IronTeams downloads Teams’ notification and ringtone sounds from Microsoft’s CDN, and the Video effects panel downloads Teams’ background pictures. They are not part of IronTeams and not shipped with it. Turn the sounds download off in Settings › Notifications and IronTeams plays its own sounds instead.

Why was it called kelma?

That was the earlier name. The repository was MohamedEslam04/kelma before it became teams-in-rust. Your old config, data and sign-in move over on the first start.

The installer is not signed. Is that a problem?

Windows SmartScreen and macOS Gatekeeper ask before the first install because the installers are not signed yet. You can check each download against the SHA-256 shown in the download list, and the source is public.
