Teams chat in about 60 MB.

IronTeams is a native desktop client for Microsoft Teams, written in Rust. The Teams web app takes around 1 GB of memory. IronTeams uses a fraction of that.

What it does

The Teams you use every day, in a familiar layout, as a small native program.

Chat
1:1, group, meeting and self-notes chats. Rich text both ways, @mentions, replies, forwarding, images, link previews, voice messages and videos. Copy, open, download or share a message's pictures and files from its right-click menu. Arabic, English and mixed-direction text, each line of a message in its own direction, also in the message Teams shows. Notify when available for the person of a 1:1 chat.
Teams and channels
Posts as cards with replies, announcements and channel files, plus a page to discover, join and manage teams.
Calendar
Day, week and month views, the event form with recurrence, the scheduling assistant and rooms.
OneDrive
Browse, upload, share and use the recycle bin, with a native PDF and Office preview.
Calls and meetings
A native call window with pre-join, meeting controls and the roster, and 1:1 incoming and outgoing call screens. Noise suppression that also removes keyboard and mouse clicks, a 720p camera that adapts to your machine, and background blur or pictures. A People panel with search, an invite link and who was invited but has not joined. Click a participant for their profile card; the speaker strip scrolls to show everyone. Join without audio, or turn the sound off when you are also in the call on another device.
Search
Messages, files, people and meetings, find in chat, and quick views for Mentions, Followed threads and Saved.
Sign-in
Sign in with a device code, which works with MFA. The refresh token stays in your system keyring, never in a file. Several accounts and guest organizations.
On your desktop
A tray icon with an unread badge, close to tray, autostart, desktop notifications, and real-time updates with automatic reconnect.
Private
No telemetry and nothing uploaded. “Report a problem” writes a local, redacted file that you read and attach yourself.
Free software
GPL-3.0-or-later, with the source on GitHub. The README lists every feature.

    IronTeams talks to the same internal endpoints the Teams web client uses. They are undocumented and Microsoft can change them at any time, which would break IronTeams. Your organization’s policies may not allow third-party clients, so check with your IT team before you use IronTeams with a work account. Use it at your own risk.

    On first run IronTeams downloads Microsoft Teams’ notification and ringtone sounds from Microsoft’s CDN, and its Video effects panel downloads Teams’ background pictures the same way. They are not part of IronTeams and not shipped with it. You can turn the sounds download off in Settings › Notifications.
