# video

## Overview
Video we send and receive. Screen sharing and the camera are captured by a `gst-launch-1.0` child process (fed from the ScreenCast or Camera portal on Linux), encoded to H.264 with OpenH264 and handed to the media session. Incoming H.264 is depacketized and decoded to RGB for display. On Linux, the RemoteDesktop portal injects a controller's input into a shared screen, and a wlr-layer-shell overlay draws the controller's cursor. Most of the directory is feature-gated (`screen-share`, `camera`, `effects`).

## Interface
- `mod.rs`: `VideoRole {MainVideo, ApplicationSharing}` (`label`, `from_label`, `modality`, `sending_direction`, `negotiation_suffix`), `I420Frame` (`new`, `len_for`, `planes`, `planes_mut`), `RgbFrame`, `i420_to_rgb` (point-samples by `step`), `halve(&I420Frame) -> I420Frame` (half width and height, 2x2 blocks averaged), `box_downscale(&I420Frame, max_width) -> Cow<I420Frame>` (area-average shrink to at most `max_width`, `halve` at ratio 2, borrowed when it already fits — used on the receive path so a downscaled picture does not alias), `step_for`, `tap`, `fit_within`, and (`screen-share`) `pump(frames, encoder, sender, keyframes)`. Modules: `h264` and `receive` always; `camera` (`camera`); `control_overlay`, `portal`, `remote_input`, `screencast` (`screen-share`); `effects` (`effects`, see [./effects/EGM.md](./effects/EGM.md)); private `gst`.
- `h264.rs`: `PAYLOADS` (107 `42c01f`, 108 `42e01f`), `fmtp`, `split_annexb`, `nal_type`, `is_keyframe`, `EncoderSettings {fps, bitrate_bps, screen, keyframe_interval, bt601}` (`screen()`, `camera()`, `camera_low()`), `CameraTier {Full, Half, Small}` (`settings`, `shrinks`), `Governor` (`new`, `tier`, `due`, `observe`), `EncodedFrame`, and (`screen-share`) `H264Encoder::{new, camera, tier, request_keyframe, encode}`. `H264Encoder::camera()` is an encoder that follows the governor: it skips frames at the lower tiers, halves them with `video::halve` at `Small` and reopens the encoder on a tier change; `tier()` is `None` for an encoder made with `new`.
- `receive.rs`: `VideoPacket` (with a `keyframe: KeyframeRequest` handle), `KeyframeRequest {request, take}` (shared by the packets of a track: the decoder asks, the track reader sends the PLI), `carries_keyframe`, `H264Depacketizer::{push, dropped}`, `LossKeyframe::{new, on_loss}` (debounced keyframe request when an access unit is dropped), `ReceiveStats::{new, packet, frame, au_dropped, queue_full, pli, sample}` and `StatsSample` (per-line receive stats with an injected clock), and (`camera`) `H264Decoder::{new, decode}` `spawn_decoder(rx, mid, max_width) -> Receiver<RgbFrame>` and `spawn_decoder_sized(rx, mid, Arc<AtomicUsize>)` (the caller may change the width while it runs; read per picture).
- `gst.rs` (`screen-share`, private): `GST_LAUNCH_ENV` (`IRONTEAMS_GST_LAUNCH`), `gst_launch`, `spawn`, `GstFrames`.
- `portal.rs`: `set_host_app_id`, `ensure_registered` (crate-private, Linux).
- `screencast.rs` (`screen-share`): `ShareKind {Screen, Window, Any}`, `CaptureSettings` (default 1280x720 at 15 fps), `ScreenCapture::{take_frames, stop}`, `pick(kind, settings) -> Result<Option<ScreenCapture>>`, `gstreamer_args`, `native_gstreamer_args`.
- `camera.rs` (`camera`): `CameraSource`, `CameraChoice`, `SystemCamera`, `CameraSettings` (default 640x360 at 15 fps, used for video clips; `CameraSettings::call()` is 1280x720 at 30 fps, which a call uses), `CameraCapture::{take_frames, stop}`, `open`, `open_choice`, `gstreamer_args`, `camera_sources`, `pipewire_camera_target`, `system_cameras`, `parse_device_monitor`.
- `remote_input.rs` (`screen-share`): `Target`, `control_target`, `InputInjector` (`pointer_motion_absolute`, `pointer_button`, `axis`, `axis_discrete`, `keyboard_keycode`, `keyboard_keysym`, `inject`, `close`; Linux only), `request_control(shared) -> Result<Option<InputInjector>>`, `UNSUPPORTED`.
- `control_overlay.rs` (`screen-share`): `ShareArea`, `OutputGeom`, `Rect`, `place`, `cursor_margin`, `label_text`, `Layout`, `layout`, `Photo`, `ControlOverlay::{show, cursor}`.
- Child contract: [./effects/EGM.md](./effects/EGM.md).

## Invariants
- `I420Frame::new` needs non-zero even dimensions and exactly `width * height * 3 / 2` bytes [mod.rs:I420Frame::new].
- H.264 is constrained baseline level 3.1 at payload types 107 and 108 with `packetization-mode=1`; the encoder inserts SPS/PPS before every IDR, starts with a keyframe and skips frames to hold the bitrate. Screen: 15 fps, 1.5 Mbps, keyframe interval 30. Camera (`CameraTier::Full`): 720p at 30 fps, 2 Mbit/s (the media server's answer allows `max-br=2000`), keyframe every 2 s (60 frames), BT.601 VUI (`bt601`). `camera_low()` (640x360, 15 fps, 600 kbit/s, selected by `IRONTEAMS_CAMERA_QUALITY=low` outside this crate) and tier `Small` use 600 kbit/s [h264.rs:PAYLOADS, H264Encoder::new, EncoderSettings, CameraTier].
- The governor steps the camera tier down when encoding takes more than 70% of the frame interval on average (not before 20 frames at a tier), and tries a tier up after 300 frames when its cost would be below 35% of that tier's interval [h264.rs:Governor::observe, TOO_BUSY, QUIET, SETTLE_FRAMES, TRY_UP_FRAMES].
- The depacketizer drops a unit on a sequence gap, an FU-A without start, or an unsupported NAL type; only marker packets emit a unit. A change of SSRC resets sequence tracking, a change of timestamp discards the unfinished unit, and padding-only (empty payload) packets count in the sequence [receive.rs:H264Depacketizer::push].
- `spawn_decoder` follows only the first track that carries a keyframe, and asks for a keyframe for packets of other tracks until one is chosen, after every decode failure, and when the depacketizer drops an access unit (a concealed loss is not a decode error, so it would otherwise persist until the next scheduled keyframe; debounced to once per 500 ms by `LossKeyframe`). A decoded picture wider than `max_width` is area-averaged down with `box_downscale`, not point-sampled, so it does not alias. The decoder lives on its own thread because it is not `Send`. It logs its receive stats (`ReceiveStats`) per line every 5 s and `remote video decoding` on the first picture and each decoded-size change [receive.rs:spawn_decoder].
- GStreamer frames are fixed-size I420 read with `read_exact`; the frame channel holds 2 frames and slow consumers lose frames [gst.rs:FRAME_QUEUE, spawn].
- The host app id is set once (first call wins) and the portal registration happens once before any other portal call; only Linux registers [portal.rs:APP_ID, REGISTERED].
- Screen capture asks the portal for an embedded cursor, a single source and no persistence; its output size is the source size fitted within `max_size` [screencast.rs:select_sources, capture_stream].
- Camera: the portal consent is asked first and a user refusal (`camera access refused`) ends the attempt with no V4L2 fallback. If the portal is missing, sees no camera or errors before the request, `portal()` returns `Portal::NoCamera` and V4L2 is used. `camera_sources` lists V4L2 devices first (chosen, else first found, else `/dev/video0`) and the portal remote after them; in Flatpak with the portal granted only the portal source is used. A source fails after 4 s without a frame, but the last source is kept when it is only slow [camera.rs:portal, open_sources, FIRST_FRAME, camera_sources].
- Remote control: the shared picture must map into the control stream (`control_target`), otherwise the request fails; user cancellation or a missing pointer grant yields `Ok(None)`. On Windows and macOS `request_control` returns an error and `InputInjector` is uninhabited [remote_input.rs:control_target, request_control].
- The overlay is a click-through `Layer::Overlay` surface (namespace `ironteams-control-cursor`, no keyboard interactivity); it needs wlr-layer-shell and wl_shm, and a failure only logs because control works without it. Off Linux, `ControlOverlay::show` is a no-op [control_overlay.rs:run, ControlOverlay::show].
- Feature gating: `camera` needs `camera`; `gst`, `screencast`, `remote_input`, `control_overlay`, `portal` and `pump` need `screen-share`; `effects` needs `effects` [mod.rs].

## Side Effects
- Spawns `gst-launch-1.0` (path from `IRONTEAMS_GST_LAUNCH`, else the installed GStreamer, else `PATH`) with `kill_on_drop`, and tasks that log its stderr and read its stdout [gst.rs:spawn]; also `pw-dump` (3 s timeout) and `gst-device-monitor-1.0` [camera.rs:pw_dump, system_cameras].
- D-Bus portal calls through `ashpd`: Registry, ScreenCast, Camera and RemoteDesktop (the latter injects real input into the desktop) [portal.rs, screencast.rs, camera.rs, remote_input.rs].
- Wayland connection and wlr-layer-shell surface on a `control-cursor` thread; runs `fc-match` and reads Noto or DejaVu font files for the label [control_overlay.rs:run, load_font].
- `pump` spawns an encode and send task; `spawn_decoder` a thread named `ironteams-video-decode`; `tap` a forwarder task.
- Reads `/.flatpak-info`; reads Windows/macOS GStreamer location env vars and paths [camera.rs:open_sources, gst.rs].
- `tracing` logs at info, warn and debug levels: `gst-launch-1.0` stderr lines at debug, the camera encoder's tier changes at info, and the remote decoder's "remote video decoding" (first picture and each decoded-size change, with decoded/drawn size and step), "remote video stats" (per line every 5 s: decoded/drawn WxH, step, fps, kbit/s, packets, lost, au_dropped, queue_full, plis) and "remote video not decoding" (at most every 5 s) at info [gst.rs:spawn, h264.rs:H264Encoder::adapt, receive.rs:spawn_decoder].
